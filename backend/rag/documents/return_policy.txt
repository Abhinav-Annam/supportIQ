SHOPEASE RETURN POLICY

1. Customers can request a return within
   7 days of product delivery.

2. Products must be returned with original
   packaging and accessories.

3. Damaged products can be reported within
   48 hours of delivery.

4. Customers should provide their order ID
   and photographs of the damaged product.

5. Return requests are subject to verification
   by the ShopEase support team.

6. Certain products may be non-returnable.
